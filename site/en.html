I Wake Up Every Morning
in Order to Dream

A dream diary for iPhone

App Store|1 month free

For you

Every night you live a few hours no one else sees. Dream keeps them. As the dreams you tell meet the days you live, a shape begins to appear — what weighs on you, what you long for, what you have not yet put into words.

For everyone

Each dream you choose to share, without your name, joins a record kept by everyone who uses Dream. Over time it becomes a large, shared record of human dreaming — a way to ask again, together, what dreams are.

How to use

When you wakeDream

Tap Dream and tell what you saw, as it comes. Stop talking and it’s saved.

During the dayNow

Open the app and murmur how you feel. It’s already listening. You can type too.

Kept for youDay

Plans, places, photos and sleep gather on their own. See them in the timeline.

The next morningRead

Open the night’s dream: Story shows it tidied, with its film and links to your day. Freud reads it and talks with you.

Over timeLook back

The dream shelf and reports show what keeps returning.

The more you leave, the deeper the reading

Dreams are woven from your days. The more you murmur with Now, and the more you let Dream see — people you meet, places you go, your plans, photos and sleep — the more links it finds, and the sharper Freud’s reading becomes. Everything you allow is used only to read your dreams, and never leaves your iPhone.

You decide what stays private. Sharing is always optional. Dream is not a medical service.
